Pre-Release Deployment Risk Monitor

Prevent production outages before broad rollout. Evaluates progressive canary telemetry in real-time, tripping automated circuit breakers and generating immutable audit dossiers.

domain 1. The Real-World Use Case

Target Industries: Banks, Healthcare Providers, Government Agencies, and FinTech where release failures trigger regulatory fines and operational disruption.

The Problem: Traditional CI/CD passes unit tests in staging, but subtle memory leaks, latency spikes, and error deltas only emerge under live traffic.
The Solution: This monitor gates releases during the Canary phase (10% → 25% traffic), ensuring harmful versions are halted before reaching 100% of customers.
