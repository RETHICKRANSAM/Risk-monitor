What is the Use Case & How Does It Work?

account_balance 1. The Enterprise Use Case

Designed for Banking, Healthcare, and Government software rollouts. In these domains, production bugs violate compliance (SOX 404, PCI-DSS) and cost millions. This monitor catches errors before releases touch 100% of customers.

share 2. Progressive Canary Gating

Traffic starts in a 25% Canary Ring alongside the stable baseline. The multi-factor engine monitors latency deltas, error budget burn, and error rates, deciding automatically whether to promote or roll back.

gavel 3. Automated Decision Gate
🟢 ALLOW (0–39 pts): Promote canary to 100%.
🟡 PAUSE (40–59 pts): Hold canary; alert SRE.
🔴 BLOCK (60+ pts): Instant circuit-breaker rollback.

Automated Gating Decision System

Pre-release governance rules mapped directly to cumulative telemetry risk scores.

Active tier pulses
ALLOW Score: 0–39
Promote canary to full production

All telemetry within SLA boundaries. Canary traffic increments progressively (25% → 50% → 100%) without human intervention.

PAUSE Score: 40–59
Hold deployment & require human review

Warning drift or degraded SLO observed. Rollout frozen in current ring. Release engineer must review evidence before promotion.

BLOCK Score: 60–100
Automatically rollback the release

Circuit breaker trips instantaneously. Canary traffic is immediately routed back to stable baseline. 0% broad customer impact.
